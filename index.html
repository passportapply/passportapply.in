Common Passport Requirements

Requirements can vary depending on your circumstances. Get guidance before submitting your application.

🧑 Adult Passport

  • Identity proof
  • Address proof
  • Date of birth proof
  • Existing passport, if applicable
  • Other documents depending on case

👶 Minor Passport

  • Child's documents
  • Parents' documents
  • Parent passport details, where applicable
  • Address proof
  • Additional documents depending on case

⚡ Tatkaal Passport

  • Required identity documents
  • Address proof
  • Additional Tatkaal documents
  • Existing passport, if applicable
  • Case-specific requirements
